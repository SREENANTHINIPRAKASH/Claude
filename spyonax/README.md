# SPYONAX Sector Concepts

*From Earth to Markets to Space.* Companion to the [SPYONAX Group Blueprint](https://claude.ai/artifact/FGWnhh76tXPnG2i9nNkMMx) (Working draft 1, 26 September 2026). Updated 27 September 2026.

The strongest business concept for each of the ten sectors, how they connect, and the order to build them in.

> **The one idea.** Every sector starts with a data product built on SPYONAX DATA. That data makes TRADING smarter, TRADING's profits pay for the physical builds, and satellites close the loop by watching the ground.

## From Earth to Markets to Space

- **Earth** (real assets and ground truth): ENERGY, AGRI, INDUSTRIES, LAND, MINING, MEDICAL
  - ↓ Ground data, sensors and collateral
- **Markets** (data becomes signals, capital and fees): TRADING, TECHNOLOGY, FINANCE
  - ↓ Profits and fees pay for the long builds
- **Space** (sees every sector from orbit): SPACE
- ↺ **Space back to Earth:** EARTH OBSERVATION watches crops, mines, sites and grids.

## The ten concepts

### Earth

#### SPYONAX ENERGY · Resources pillar · #4 to build · Forecasting Phase 2–3 · plants Phase 4

**Forecast power before generating it. SPYONAX GRID sells solar and wind generation forecasts built with QRL's forecasting methods and satellite cloud data. SPYONAX SOLAR builds plants on LAND sites once buyers have signed.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX GRID, SPYONAX SOLAR, SPYONAX ENERGY STORAGE
- **Customers:** Wind and solar generators, who must submit generation schedules and pay for deviations from them. Later, industrial buyers of green power.
- **Money:** Forecasting fees per megawatt. Later, long-term power purchase agreements.
- **Group edge:** Forecasting generation is a time-series problem like the ones QRL already solves. EARTH OBSERVATION adds cloud imagery for short-range solar forecasts.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. Backtest a day-ahead forecast for two or three solar and wind sites with public output data.
  2. Compare its error with the deviation bands generators are charged on.
  3. Pitch one generator or scheduling agency.
- **Gate to scale:** Plants (Phase 4) only when LAND has a site and a buyer has signed a power purchase agreement.
- **Key number:** Forecast error against actual output. Megawatts under forecasting contracts.
- **Biggest risk:** Established forecasting firms. Later, slow payments and open-access rules that differ by state.
- **Check with counsel:** Forecasting, scheduling and deviation rules of the central and state electricity commissions. Open-access rules for selling power to industry.
- **Feeds:** LAND, INDUSTRIES, TRADING

#### SPYONAX AGRI · Life pillar · #5 to build · AGRITECH Phase 3 (can start in 2)

**Software before farmland. SPYONAX AGRITECH gives farmer producer organisations, insurers and lenders field-level crop intelligence, and collects the ground truth that makes EARTH OBSERVATION accurate.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX AGRITECH, SPYONAX COLD CHAIN (next)
- **Customers:** Farmer producer organisations (FPOs), crop insurers, agri lenders and input companies. Farmers get advice free on WhatsApp, in their own language.
- **Money:** Per-hectare, per-season fees paid by the institution.
- **Group edge:** EARTH OBSERVATION, DATA and AI LAB are already in place. Each season's harvest data makes the models better, and no one can buy that history.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. One FPO, 500 to 1,000 farmers, one crop, one district.
  2. Collect geotagged field photos, sowing dates and harvest yields.
  3. Ask for a paid pilot from the start.
- **Gate to scale:** Paying customers for two seasons. Then use the post-harvest loss data to choose where COLD CHAIN pays.
- **Key number:** Paid hectares. Yield-forecast error against the actual harvest.
- **Biggest risk:** Farmers rarely pay for advice, so charge institutions instead.
- **Check with counsel:** DPDP Act, 2023 consent for farmer data. FSSAI and APEDA later, for foods and exports.
- **Feeds:** SPACE, FINANCE, TRADING

#### SPYONAX INDUSTRIES · Resources pillar · #7 to build · Design Phase 3–4 · factory Phase 4

**Design the electronics the group needs first: field sensors, battery management and power electronics. Sell them to outside buyers once they work inside the group.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX ELECTRONICS, SPYONAX MANUFACTURING (later)
- **Customers:** AGRI, ENERGY, LAND and SPACE inside the group. Outside equipment makers later.
- **Money:** Product sales and design services.
- **Group edge:** Guaranteed internal demand gives the first volume. Government electronics incentive schemes support manufacturing in India.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. One product: a low-cost sensor node for fields and warehouses.
  2. Design it in-house and build it with a contract manufacturer.
  3. Pilot it with AGRITECH and WAREHOUSING.
- **Gate to scale:** An own factory only with steady outside orders and an INDUSTRIAL PARKS site ready.
- **Key number:** Units shipped. Gross margin per unit.
- **Biggest risk:** Hardware ties up cash, and cheap imports set the price.
- **Check with counsel:** BIS certification for regulated electronics. A factory licence and pollution-control consent for an own plant.
- **Feeds:** AGRI, ENERGY, LAND, SPACE

#### SPYONAX LAND · Resources pillar · #8 to build · Phase 3

**Build a land bank that earns while it waits. SPYONAX WAREHOUSING and INDUSTRIAL PARKS collect rent now and hold the sites that SOLAR and INDUSTRIES will need later.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX WAREHOUSING, SPYONAX INDUSTRIAL PARKS
- **Customers:** Logistics, e-commerce and manufacturing tenants, plus the group's own ENERGY, INDUSTRIES and AGRI cold chain.
- **Money:** Lease rent, plus land value over time.
- **Group edge:** Site selection from EARTH OBSERVATION and DATA, covering roads, substations and flood risk. The group can be its own anchor tenant.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. A site-selection model on satellite and public data, before buying anything.
  2. One build-to-suit warehouse with a tenant signed before construction starts.
- **Gate to scale:** The Blueprint's gate of surplus capital, plus a signed tenant for every building.
- **Key number:** Occupancy. Rental yield on cost.
- **Biggest risk:** Capital-heavy and slow, with a risk of land-title disputes.
- **Check with counsel:** Title checks, land-use conversion approvals, state limits on buying agricultural land, and RERA for anything sold to buyers.
- **Feeds:** ENERGY, INDUSTRIES, AGRI

#### SPYONAX MINING · Resources pillar · #9 to build · Data Phase 4 · mines Phase 6

**Find deposits and sell the rights. SPYONAX EXPLORATION maps critical-mineral targets from satellite and geological data and wins exploration licences. Partners run the mines.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX EXPLORATION, SPYONAX CRITICAL MINERALS
- **Customers:** Companies bidding in critical-mineral block auctions. Later, the buyers of licensed discoveries.
- **Money:** Prospect reports first. Later, a share of the value when a deposit found under an exploration licence goes to auction.
- **Group edge:** EARTH OBSERVATION, AI LAB and MATERIALS LAB, plus open geoscience data from the Geological Survey of India.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. A prospect model for one critical mineral in one geological belt, built from public data.
  2. Sell the report to one auction bidder.
- **Gate to scale:** Mine operation (Phase 6) only with exploration capital, identified blocks, and an operating partner.
- **Key number:** Targets confirmed by drilling. Paying bidders.
- **Biggest risk:** Exploration succeeds rarely and takes years.
- **Check with counsel:** The 2023 amendment to the MMDR Act created exploration licences for critical and deep-seated minerals, so confirm the current terms. Forest and environmental clearances.
- **Feeds:** INDUSTRIES, ENERGY

#### SPYONAX MEDICAL · Life pillar · #10 to build · Phase 5, through VENTURES

**Enter through VENTURES before building anything. Take a stake in, or partner with, an Indian AI-diagnostics company, and build SPYONAX DIAGNOSTICS in-house only after hiring a medical leader.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX DIAGNOSTICS
- **Customers:** Diagnostic labs, hospitals and government screening programmes.
- **Money:** Per-scan or per-site software licences.
- **Group edge:** AI LAB and BIO LAB. This is the sector least connected to the Earth, Markets and Space loop, which is why a light entry fits.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. Map the CDSCO route for diagnostic software, which is regulated as a medical device.
  2. Shortlist three to five AI-diagnostics startups for a VENTURES stake.
  3. Pick one narrow screening use case.
- **Gate to scale:** The Blueprint's gate: medical and regulatory leaders hired, and the CDSCO route mapped.
- **Key number:** CDSCO-approved products. Scans per month.
- **Biggest risk:** Long approvals, clinical liability, and strong incumbents.
- **Check with counsel:** CDSCO rules for software as a medical device. Clinical-trial and ethics-committee approvals.
- **Feeds:** CAPITAL

### Markets

#### SPYONAX TRADING · Capital pillar · #1 to build · Now · Phase 1

**Run every bot as one prop desk. QRL researches strategies, a fixed gate decides which go live, and capital goes only to strategies that keep passing it.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX QUANT, SPYONAX ALGO (internal)
- **Customers:** No outside customers. The desk trades group capital only, as the Blueprint requires.
- **Money:** Trading profit on group capital.
- **Group edge:** Your bots already run on Dhan, Delta Exchange and Binance. QRL's research streams (microstructure, options, volatility, regimes) give all of them one validation process.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. One risk engine across every bot: exposure, margin, and a group-wide daily loss limit with a kill switch.
  2. A strategy register with a four-step gate: backtest, paper, small live, scaled.
  3. Monthly profit attribution by desk and strategy. Retire any strategy that fails its gate.
  4. Separate books for Indian F&O and crypto, because they are taxed differently.
- **Gate to scale:** 12 months of audited live results inside the drawdown limit. That is the Blueprint's trigger for Phase 2 and FINANCE.
- **Key number:** Return per unit of maximum drawdown, by strategy. Share of capital in strategies that passed the gate.
- **Biggest risk:** Backtests that fail live. Treat every new strategy as unproven until it passes paper and small live.
- **Check with counsel:** RBI's principal-business (50-50) test, as the Blueprint notes. Crypto gains are taxed at a flat 30% with 1% TDS.
- **Feeds:** CAPITAL, FINANCE, TECHNOLOGY

#### SPYONAX TECHNOLOGY · Technology pillar · #2 to build · Now · Phase 1

**Build one data spine for the group. SPYONAX DATA collects once, every sector and QRL reads from it, and SPYONAX API sells the parts outsiders will pay for.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX DATA, SPYONAX API, SPYONAX AI
- **Customers:** TRADING first. Then quant developers, researchers and fintechs who want clean derivatives history through an API, with no recommendations attached.
- **Money:** API subscriptions and dataset licences.
- **Group edge:** Your data bots already collect crypto options and futures data. A long, clean history is hard for anyone else to copy.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. One schema and one store for every bot's data, with automated quality checks.
  2. An internal API that TRADING and QRL use for all research.
  3. Pick one dataset with outside demand, such as crypto options history with implied volatility, and package it with documentation.
  4. Read each exchange's terms before reselling its data.
- **Gate to scale:** A first paying API customer within 9 months of launch. Otherwise keep DATA internal.
- **Key number:** Number of sectors reading from SPYONAX DATA. External annual recurring revenue.
- **Biggest risk:** Building a platform no outsider pays for. Keep the external product narrow.
- **Check with counsel:** Exchange data licences and API terms (NSE data needs a licence to redistribute). DPDP Act, 2023 for any personal data.
- **Feeds:** All sectors

#### SPYONAX FINANCE · Capital pillar · #6 to build · Phase 2, when the gate is met

**Offer QRL's proven strategies to outside investors through a regulated quant fund, and only after the group's own money has proven them.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX ASSET MANAGEMENT, SPYONAX WEALTH (later)
- **Customers:** High-net-worth investors, family offices and institutions who meet SEBI's minimum investment.
- **Money:** Management fee and performance fee.
- **Group edge:** An audited live record from TRADING, run on the same risk engine the fund will use.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. Brief counsel on the vehicle. A Category III AIF is the usual choice for derivatives-heavy quant strategies, and PMS is the alternative.
  2. Get TRADING's records audited.
  3. Write rules for conflicts between the prop desk and the fund, such as which book gets a trade first.
- **Gate to scale:** The Blueprint's Phase 2 gate: an audited record, an agreed licence route, and capital for the net-worth requirement. Most outside investors want two to three years of record.
- **Key number:** Assets under management. Fee income against compliance cost.
- **Biggest risk:** Compliance costs arrive before assets do.
- **Check with counsel:** SEBI registration for the fund or PMS, in its own licensed entity. Hold words like Asset Management until the licence path is clear (rule N8).
- **Feeds:** CAPITAL

### Space

#### SPYONAX SPACE · Technology pillar · #3 to build · EO Phase 2 · satellites Phase 7

**Sell satellite answers before building satellites. SPYONAX EARTH OBSERVATION turns bought-in imagery into crop, mine, site and grid intelligence. SPYONAX SATELLITES follows only when customers need data nobody sells.**

- **Flagship:** SPYONAX EARTH OBSERVATION, SPYONAX SATELLITES (later)
- **Customers:** AGRI, MINING, LAND, ENERGY and QRL inside the group. Crop insurers, commodity traders, lenders and state agencies outside it.
- **Money:** Analytics subscriptions, per-area reports, and access through SPYONAX API.
- **Group edge:** Ground truth from AGRITECH calibrates models for India's small fields, and QRL tests which signals actually move markets.
- **First 90 days:**
  1. One district and one crop, using free Sentinel-2 optical imagery plus Sentinel-1 radar, which sees through monsoon cloud.
  2. Acreage and crop-health maps, checked against official estimates.
  3. Show the maps to one insurer and one lender.
- **Gate to scale:** Satellites (Phase 7) only with paying demand that commercial imagery can't meet, IN-SPACe authorisation, and the capital to build.
- **Key number:** Accuracy against official crop estimates. Number of paying EO customers.
- **Biggest risk:** General satellite analytics is crowded. Win on India-specific ground truth and narrow questions.
- **Check with counsel:** IN-SPACe authorisation under the Indian Space Policy, 2023 for satellites. India's geospatial guidelines and each imagery provider's licence terms.
- **Feeds:** AGRI, MINING, LAND, ENERGY, TRADING

## Changes I'd make to the Blueprint

Each of these is your call. None changes a name or a naming rule.

1. **Commit EARTH OBSERVATION to Phase 2.** The Blueprint lets it begin early. Make it a commitment, because it needs no land or licence and it serves QRL as well as AGRI.
2. **Give ENERGY a data entry in Phase 2 or 3.** Generation forecasting under SPYONAX GRID uses the same skills as QRL and needs no land.
3. **Bring MEDICAL in through SPYONAX VENTURES first.** It is the sector with the weakest link to the rest of the group and the heaviest regulation.
4. **Set MINING's goal as discovery.** Win exploration licences and let partners run the mines.
5. **Write a capital policy for SPYONAX CAPITAL.** Sweep a fixed share of TRADING's realised profit to Holdings each quarter, keep 12 months of group runway in cash, and release capital to a sector only when its gate is met.
